Feeding Management

Record feedings and automatically calculate the next feeding date. Get reminders so you never miss a meal.

📈

Growth Tracking

Track weight and body length over time with visual graphs. Monitor your pet's healthy development.

Record bulb replacement dates and get notified when it's time for a new one.

Calendar View

See all care events on a calendar. Quickly review past records and upcoming schedules.

Photo Album

Capture growth moments with photos. Browse your pet's visual timeline anytime.
